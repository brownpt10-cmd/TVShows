Turning this into a real Amazon Music playlist

Amazon Music doesn't offer a way for outside apps (or a page like this) to build a playlist for you automatically — there's no consumer-facing API, and Amazon's own "Import Your Playlists" feature only pulls a playlist in from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, SoundCloud, or Deezer. It doesn't accept a plain song list or CSV directly.

The closest thing to one-click automation:

  1. Download the CSV(s) below.
  2. Go to TuneMyMusic and choose the CSV → Amazon Music transfer (free for this size list).
  3. Upload the CSV, sign in and authorize Amazon Music when prompted, and it matches each track by title/artist and builds the playlist for you — you'll get a report of anything it couldn't match.

For one-off listening (not building a saved playlist), every "Listen" button in the tables above opens a live search on Amazon Music for that exact song — click it, then hit play or add it to a playlist yourself from there.
